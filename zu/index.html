Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ngidinga uhlelo lokusebenza uma sengisebenzisa ibhothi?

Cha. Ibhothi le-Telegram liyimikhiqizo esebenza ngokugcwele ngokwalo. Uhlelo lokusebenza lweselula lungeza izikhumbuzo zokucindezela, imodi engaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi, ukufinyelela kwesikrini sasekhaya nezibalo zomdabu — kodwa zonke izici eziyinhloko zisebenza ku-Telegram futhi. Sebenzisa noma yini ekufanele kahle; idatha iyahambisana.

Ingabe kukhona uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-iOS?

Akukabi khona — uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-Android lukhona ku-Google Play, i-iOS isendleleni. Okwamanje, ibhothi le-Telegram lisebenza kahle ku-iPhone.

Yiziphi izilimi ezisekelwayo?

Isikhombimsebenzisi: isiNgisi, isiSpanishi, isiRashiya nesiZulu. Ukuqaphela ukudla kanye nomqeqeshi we-AI kuqonda ukufaka ngolimi oluthile.

Idatha yami isetshenziswa kanjani?

Sigcina umlando wakho wezidlo, isisindo nezilinganiso ukuze sikunikeze isiqondiso esenziwe ngokwezifiso. Idatha yokukhokha icutshungulwa umhlinzeki wethu wokukhokha — asikaze sibone izinombolo zamakhadi. Bheka Inqubomgomo Yobumfihlo yethu.

Ngingakwazi ukukhansela noma nini?

Yebo. Ukuchofoza okukodwa kuhlelo lokusebenza noma umyalezo wokusekela. Inqubomgomo yokubuyisela imali iyatholakala lapha.
